## Bug found and fixed (real, not cosmetic)

The WIP had `root.z_index = -1` on pump roots ("behind the player, like the
town buildings"). That put every pump **underneath** the per-pool murk tint
Polygon2D in `skin.gd` (z 4) and the water walls (z 0) — pumps were rendering,
fully positioned and textured, but 100% invisible in game, always, at every
level. Confirmed by instrumenting build_pump with prints + a bright modulate
override (see git history if needed) that walked through position, texture,
visibility, and z math — nothing else was wrong. Fixed by matching the old
(pre-v3) pump builder's `z_index = 3`; bumped to `5` to clear the murk tint
too. Also added a small `modulate = Color(1.35, 1.28, 1.18)` lift on the
pump sprite: the baked rust-brown palette is otherwise very low-contrast
against the pit's near-black interior (see "what's still old" below).

## What's still old / needs another pass

- **Pump contrast.** Even at z 5 with the modulate lift, the pump housing's
  rust-brown/black palette nearly matches the pit's near-black interior and
  the treeline shadow at the rim. This is the same root cause as the water
  track's #1 item ("water reads as invisible dark mud") and the
  critters+night track's night-darkness item — a props-side palette tweak
  can only do so much while the basin itself renders near-black. Once
  water/night lighten the basin, pumps will likely read fine without
  further changes; flag for a joint look after those land.
- **Wanted poster** was not visually verified in-game: it only shows once
  `GameManager.is_swamp_completed(4)` is true, and there's no debug env hook
  for it (unlike pumps/camels/island/heli). The bake looks correct in
  isolation (parchment texture, "WANTED" header, figure silhouette); a
  real playthrough or a save-state test should confirm the in-game read.
- **Helicopter** second rotor frame has a slightly stray diagonal line from
  the connected-components frame split merging a tail-rotor fragment at an
  odd angle. Minor, only visible on close zoom; not worth a re-gen given
  the tight per-track budget.
